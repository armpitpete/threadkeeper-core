# Decision Context v1

An accepted decision does not erase the reasoning landscape that preceded it.

Decision context may preserve alternatives considered, reasons rejected, attributable dissent, unresolved uncertainty and explicit reopening conditions. These records are evidence about the decision process, not competing authority transitions by themselves.

A future client should be able to answer both "what was accepted?" and "why were the serious alternatives rejected, and what would justify reopening the decision?" without depending on an old conversation.
