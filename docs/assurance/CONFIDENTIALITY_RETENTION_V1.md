# Confidentiality and Retention v1

Core content is classified independently of authority as `public`, `internal`, `confidential` or `restricted`. A client may retrieve content only when its clearance is at least the record classification and policy permits the requested operation.

Retention is also independent of authority. Where policy requires content destruction, Threadkeeper may retain a governed redaction/tombstone proving that a record/event existed, who authorised destruction, when content was destroyed and why, while no longer retaining the protected content itself.

Backups, escrow, federation and exports must obey the same classification and retention decision. Immutability is not permission to ignore legal or policy-mandated deletion.
